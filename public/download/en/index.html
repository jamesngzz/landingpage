Original history stays on your Mac

Your entire history is not synced to an online database. With Cloud AI enabled, relevant context is sent for analysis. Onboarding preselects private Supabase sharing of ratings, analysis context and insights to improve Wizzdom; turn either option off before continuing.

Get started in 3 steps.

The app guides you through the details. No Terminal required.

  1. 01

    Install & open Wizzdom

    Open the DMG, drag into Applications and sign in. Updates download automatically and wait for you to choose restart.

  2. 02

    Choose sources & grant local access

    When macOS requests Accessibility, grant it to Wizzdom following the app’s instructions.

  3. 03

    Connect your AI & send a turn

    Connect Codex or Claude, then check the result in Today.

macOS grants Accessibility to Wizzdom running locally, separately from Cloud AI. For Claude Chat it reads displayed chat, never drafts. Revoke it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
