Get started in 3 steps.

The app guides you through the details. No Terminal required.

  1. 01

    Drag into Applications

    Open the DMG, drag Wizzdom into Applications and launch it.

  2. 02

    Sign in & choose sources

    Choose the history you want to use and consent to analysis scope.

  3. 03

    Connect your AI & send a turn

    Connect Codex or Claude, then check the result in Today.

Claude Chat needs Accessibility for Wizzdom and only reads displayed foreground chat, never drafts. Revoke access anytime in macOS settings.
